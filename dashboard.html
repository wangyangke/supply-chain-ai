Confidence Scoring Engine (0–100)

Every relationship is scored across 5 weighted dimensions. The score determines its epistemic status: ≥70 = confirmed, 40–69 = inferred, <40 = unknown.

DimensionMaxWhat It Measures
Authority25Source credibility: SEC filing (25) > official IR (20) > business media (14) > Wikipedia (10) > blog (6)
Evidence Quality25Number of independent sources: 1 = 10, 2 = 18, 3+ = 25. Same-URL duplicates count as one.
Recency20How fresh is the evidence? <6mo = 20, <1yr = 16, <2yr = 12, <3yr = 6, >3yr = 0. Official ongoing relationships stay at 20.
Specificity20Does the evidence name both parties and the relationship type? Direct official statement = +5 bonus.
Quantifiability10Are there hard numbers (revenue %, investment $, stake %)? Both money + percentage = 10; one = 5; none = 0.
Two Refinements

1. Official Ongoing Relationship Recency Rule
If a relationship is still valid (valid_until is null) and backed by an official source (SEC filing, exchange filing, government, company IR, or company press release), recency stays at full 20 — an officially ongoing relationship is inherently fresh.

2. Direct Statement Bonus
When an official source explicitly names the counterparty in a relationship context (e.g., "Our competitors include AMD"), specificity gets +5. Third-party estimates don't qualify even if specific.
